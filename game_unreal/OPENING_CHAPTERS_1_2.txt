THE OTHER SIDE OF YESTERDAY — CHAPTERS 1–2 PLAY GUIDE

WINDOWS GAME
The standalone build is ../output/playable/Windows. Use its PlayFirstPerson.bat
or PlayThirdPerson.bat; Unreal Editor is not required. Keep the entire Windows
folder together. These launchers select the existing DirectX 11 compatibility
renderer because repeated offscreen DirectX 12 starts reported an AMD graphics
driver error on this PC. The project renderer and installed driver were not
changed. Lighting/shadows can differ from the DirectX 12 editor view.

Both public game launch paths started, rendered the authored intro in the
correct view, and remained alive for eight-second isolated offscreen checks.
Those checks used a test-only 30 FPS cap, then a supervisor stop. See
packaged_build_report.json and packaged_startup_validation.json. This is
startup/render evidence, not a complete packaged keyboard playthrough or a
long-term stability guarantee. Use the launchers rather than the default EXE
on this PC; the EXE alone retains its original DirectX 12 default.

UNREAL PROJECT
Start PlayHomeFirstPerson.bat or PlayHomeThirdPerson.bat, or open the existing

The Harbor launchers open the earlier environment instead of this story slice.

CONTROLS
WASD / mouse     Move and look with the existing desktop controls
Left Shift       Jog
Left Ctrl        Toggle crouch; a custom crouch pose is not supplied
E                Contextual interaction; advance dialogue; close a document
F                Inspect a selected photograph or letter
R                Turn over the held photograph
Left / Right     Rotate the held photograph
1 / 2 / 3        Select a displayed dialogue response
Space            Skip the three opening recollections
Q                Open / close the Chronowatch
Wheel / arrows   In the watch: up / right selects present; down / left selects past
E                In the watch: confirm travel at the courtyard gate anchor
M                In the watch: show discovered local locations
1 / 2 / 3 / 4    In the watch: select an island; only Verdant is available
P / T            In the watch: explain unavailable perspective / time-pause tests
Tab              Open / close the journal
Left / Right     In the journal: move through its three pages
Escape           Pause; 1 resumes, 2 saves committed progress, 3 loads

Dialogue waits for manual E after a minimum readable hold. Choosing a response
plays only that branch. Walking away can close a conversation without choosing
a response. The opening recollections lock movement but permit desktop look;
skipping them grants no clues. Most reading and watch views lock movement.
Unreal Editor may reserve Escape to stop PIE; the game launchers use the game menu.

CHAPTER 1
Explore the surviving family room. The photograph and unsent letter can be
examined in either order. Mother holds you as a baby in the photograph; your
father took it. R reveals the inscription, "Our first summer at home."
Finish their associated conversations, then speak with Mother. The three honest
responses have no trust penalty.

Examine the workbench after that conversation. Take the prepared contact from
the dish, fit it at the workbench, then complete two separate stable tests there.
Each test must finish and its dialogue must be advanced. Closing the test view
or pausing cancels an unfinished test while preserving committed placement.
The successful test status is RETURN SIGNAL — STABLE.

Mother walks to the courtyard gate after calibration. Reading the original
memorial beside the gate is optional. Wait for Mother, use the gate anchor and
finish the departure conversation. In the watch select Verdant before the war
and press E to confirm. Turning the dial alone does not travel. Confirmation
requires the registered gate anchor nearby and a clear view of it.

CHAPTER 2
Speak with Neri near the gate, then find the Healer at the blue-shuttered clinic.
The linen task can be declined and offered again. After accepting, speak with
Sela, physically collect the wrapped linen from her counter, and place it in
the clinic tray. Speak with the Healer again for the waterproof-cover request.

At Sela's shop, hear the trade-off: there is one sound waterproof sheet for two
possible sets of fixings. Plan for the clinic courtyard, the harbor waiting
shelter, or choose "Not yet." Planning does not grant the sheet or change history.
Collect the actual sheet and straps from the counter when ready.

Look at both sites if desired. Interacting with different fixings first changes
the plan; interacting again at the planned site installs the sheet and commits
that outcome. Read the prompt: "Install" is the committing action. The sheet is
consumed once and cannot cover both sites. After installation, report to the
Healer. The linen delivery and installation award trust once each; they do not
unlock perspective.

Return to the gate, follow the present-day return signal and confirm the era.
Read the changed historical display, then follow Mother into the surviving
family room. Complete the final conversation, explicitly choose a response and
advance its shared final lines to finish Chapter 2.

The city remains damaged. The installed cover changes one bounded community
record. If the original memorial was read, the journal preserves that prior
observation alongside the ending's latest observation. Without that earlier
reading, it does not invent a memory of the original. Journal pages 1 and 2
track Home and Verdant; page 3 (internal JournalPage 2) holds the observations.

SAVE AND RECOVERY
The existing SaveGame slot remains Yesterday_Opening_v1. Earned photo, letter,
Mother and calibration progress migrates from the earlier opening. A load uses
the courtyard's safe recovery position and restores committed quest and era
state. It does not auto-select an unfinished dialogue response. Pause-menu 2 saves
committed progress, not a half-complete test or travel transition. The ending
reports whether saving succeeded.

PRESENTATION AND SCOPE
Gameplay executes native Blueprint nodes; Python is used only to author assets.
No engine, dependency, plugin or rendering-pipeline upgrade is required by these
authoring scripts. The existing first- and third-person locomotion is reused.

Character conversations currently use readable speaker-labelled text, existing
temporary mannequin meshes and basic authored walking. There is no supplied
voice performance, lip sync, bespoke hand interaction or complete cinematic
animation system. Photo, watch, clinic, shop, cover and archive visuals include
temporary art or simple meshes. These are not final visual-production claims.
Desktop controls are the implementation target; headset interaction is unverified.

Perspective is unavailable: no supported authored viewpoint/consent system is
present, and these tasks do not force trust to 100. The optional time-pause test
is unavailable because no supported reactive-object system is present. Neither
optional feature blocks departure or completion of these two chapters.
